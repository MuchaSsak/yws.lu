This project aims to offer a rich and varied programme of sports activities for young people aged 15 to 25. Its main goal is to promote a healthy lifestyle while offering opportunities to socialise, learn and grow. The activities cover team sports, individual sports, and cultural and educational experiences, to meet the participants’ different interests and needs.

### Main objectives

Our main objective is to bring at least 50 young people together around our project.

We want these young people to have the chance to discover sports they don’t know, in a relaxed and friendly way, without pressure or judgement.

The aim is to get young people away from their screens for a few hours, so that they become aware of the world around them. It is essential that young people with a migrant background do not feel alone or rejected by society.

### Key activities

Organising sports activities such as football, volleyball, boxing, dance, cycling, running and forest walks for disadvantaged young people.

Participants are encouraged to suggest the activities they would like to do. These activities need preparation: renting a space, recruiting participants, buying equipment, and so on.

### Expected results

We expect the young people who take part to develop tolerance and integrate into our society, to enjoy good times together and to want to keep playing sport by finding one they love. We also want to strengthen their confidence in themselves and in their abilities. We hope that coming to our sessions will make them feel less lonely.

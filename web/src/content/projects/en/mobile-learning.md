This project, coordinated by Youth Work Synergy in partnership with NINFEA and KulturNest e.V., aims to develop a comprehensive guide to best practices in mobile learning for adults. Running for 14 months (from September 2024 to October 2025), it aims to strengthen the resources of the Training and Evaluation Cycle (TEC) and to promote inclusion and diversity in adult education.

### Main objectives

Create a collaborative platform where TEC practitioners can share their experience and knowledge.

Develop a guide that brings together TEC activities and resources, specifically for the integration of newcomers.

Identify and analyse best practices by consulting stakeholders from eight European countries.

### Expected results

A digital platform for sharing good practices.

A practical guide for education and integration professionals.

Stronger international collaboration and better practices for welcoming newcomers and for intercultural coexistence.

### Main activities

Project management (YWS): strategic planning, partner coordination and resource management.

Research and platform development (NINFEA): collecting data on TEC practices and creating an online platform.

Guide production (KulturNest): compiling best practices with a focus on intercultural approaches.

Dissemination (YWS/KulturNest/NINFEA): distributing the guide and organising workshops and events to share the project’s results.

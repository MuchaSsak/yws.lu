## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see the data we hold about you, to correct it or erase it, to restrict its use, and to
receive it in a portable format, and you can object to its use. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at
any time. Write to [contact@yws.lu](mailto:contact@yws.lu); we answer within one month.

You can also complain to the Commission nationale pour la protection des données (CNPD), 1, avenue du Rock'n'roll,
L-4361 Esch-sur-Alzette, tel. (+352) 26 10 60-1, [cnpd.public.lu](https://cnpd.public.lu/).

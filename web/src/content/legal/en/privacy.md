This privacy policy (the "Policy") sets out how personal data are processed in connection with the website available
at [www.yws.lu](https://www.yws.lu/) and yws.lu, including all of its pages (the "Website"). It is provided pursuant to Articles 12 to 14 of
Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 (the "GDPR"). It is an
information notice and does not constitute a contract.

## 1. Scope, legal framework and definitions

1.1. This Policy applies exclusively to the processing of personal data carried out by means of the Website. It does
not apply to:

- processing carried out by third parties on their own websites, platforms or services, including those to which the
  Website links (section 4.4);
- personal data that you provide to Youth Work Synergy through a third-party service (such as a form hosted on Google
  Forms) or otherwise than through the Website. That processing is not carried out by means of the Website and is not
  covered by this Policy; the information required by Articles 13 and 14 GDPR is provided for it separately.

1.2. The applicable framework comprises the GDPR; the _Loi du 1er août 2018 portant organisation de la Commission
nationale pour la protection des données et mise en oeuvre du règlement (UE) 2016/679 […]_; and the _Loi modifiée du
30 mai 2005 relative aux dispositions spécifiques de protection de la personne à l'égard du traitement des données à
caractère personnel dans le secteur des communications électroniques […]_.

1.3. The terms "personal data", "processing", "controller", "joint controllers", "processor", "recipient" and "third
party" have the meanings given to them in Articles 4 and 26 GDPR.

## 3. Characteristics of the Website

3.1. The Website is a static website. It contains no forms, user accounts, newsletter, chat or comment function, and it
uses no analytics, audience measurement, advertising, profiling or tracking technologies.

3.2. YWS does not, by means of the Website, store information or gain access to information already stored on your
terminal equipment: the Website sets no cookies and uses no local storage or similar technologies. The language of the
Website is part of the page address (`/en/`, `/fr/`). The fonts, images and 3D models of the Website are delivered
from the Website itself.

3.3. The only third-party content embedded in the Website is the map on the home page (section 4.2).

## 4. Processing activities

### 4.1. Hosting and delivery of the Website

(a) Data concerned: your IP address and the approximate location derived from it; information about your browser,
operating system and device; and the technical data of each request, such as the address of the page requested, the
date and time of the request, the referring page as transmitted by your browser, and the response status.

(b) Purposes: delivering the pages of the Website to your browser; ensuring the security, availability and integrity of
the Website; detecting, preventing and investigating abuse, attacks and technical faults.

(c) Legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, the legitimate interest of YWS in operating a secure and functioning website.
These data are necessary for the Website to be displayed; without them, it cannot be delivered to you.

(d) Processor: the Website is hosted by Vercel Inc., 440 N Barranca Avenue #4133 Covina, CA 91723 United States
([privacy policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy)), which processes these data on behalf of YWS as a
processor within the meaning of Article 28 GDPR. PLACEHOLDER (confirm that the Website's Vercel plan is covered by
Vercel's Data Processing Addendum).

(e) Retention: the technical data are kept for the period determined by the hosting service's log settings and no
longer than necessary for the purposes set out in point (b). They may be kept longer only where, and for as long as,
necessary to investigate a specific security incident or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

### 4.2. Embedded map (Google Maps)

(a) The contact section of the home page contains a map of YWS's office provided by Google (Google Maps). The map loads
automatically when that section is about to appear on your screen; it does not load on the other pages of the Website,
nor while only the upper part of the home page is displayed. When the map loads, your browser connects directly to
Google's servers.

(b) Data concerned: Google receives your IP address, information about your browser and device, and the address of the
page on which the map is displayed. Google may store information on your terminal equipment or access information
already stored there (for example cookies), in particular if you are signed in to a Google account.

(c) Purpose and legal basis: presenting the location of YWS's office in an interactive and easily usable form, on the
basis of the legitimate interest of YWS in doing so (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

(d) Responsibility: to the extent that YWS and Google jointly determine the purposes and means of the collection of
these data by the embedded map and of their transmission to Google, they may be joint controllers for that collection
and transmission only (Court of Justice of the European Union, judgment of 29 July 2019, _Fashion ID_, C-40/17). YWS
does not receive, and has no access to, the data so collected, and has no influence over their further processing,
for which Google alone is responsible. For users in the European Economic Area, the Google entity responsible is Google
Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Google's processing, including its retention periods
and any transfer of data outside the European Economic Area, is described in Google's
[privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

(e) Your choices: the address of YWS's office is also given in text next to the map, and the "Open in Google Maps" link
opens Google Maps only when you select it. You can prevent the map from loading by blocking third-party content in
your browser's settings or with a browser extension.

(c) Purposes and legal basis: answering and following up your request, on the basis of the legitimate interest of YWS
in responding to the persons who contact it (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR); where your request concerns steps to be taken at
your request before the conclusion of a contract (for example renting a property to YWS or a room from YWS), Article
6(1)(b) GDPR.

(d) Recipients: the members of YWS's staff and volunteers who need the information to handle your request; the provider
of YWS's email service, as processor, PLACEHOLDER (name and location of the email service provider); and the
telecommunications operators that carry the communication.

(e) Retention: for as long as necessary to handle your request and its follow-up. Correspondence that becomes part of a
contractual relationship is kept for the duration of that relationship and of the applicable limitation periods.
Otherwise it is deleted, unless longer retention is required by law or is necessary to establish, exercise or defend
legal claims.

## 8. Security

8.1. YWS implements technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risks, in particular: delivery of the
Website over encrypted connections (HTTPS); a static Website with no database and no server-side processing of data
entered by visitors; and security headers that disable access to the camera, microphone and geolocation, limit the
information transmitted to other websites as the referring page, and restrict the embedding of the Website in other
websites.

8.2. No transmission of data over the Internet can be guaranteed to be entirely secure. YWS will act in accordance with
Articles 33 and 34 GDPR in the event of a personal data breach.

## 9. Your rights

9.1. Under the conditions and within the limits laid down by the GDPR, you have the right to:

- obtain confirmation as to whether personal data concerning you are processed, and access to them (Article 15);
- have inaccurate data rectified and incomplete data completed (Article 16);
- obtain the erasure of your data (Article 17);
- obtain the restriction of processing (Article 18);
- receive the data you have provided in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format and have them
  transmitted to another controller, where the processing is based on your consent or on a contract and is carried out
  by automated means (Article 20);
- withdraw your consent at any time where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of
  processing carried out before its withdrawal (Article 7(3)).

9.2. To exercise these rights, contact YWS at the contact details in section 2. Where YWS has reasonable doubts as to
the identity of the person making a request, it may request additional information necessary to confirm it (Article
12(6) GDPR).

9.3. YWS responds without undue delay and in any event within one month of receipt of the request. That period may be
extended by two further months where necessary, taking into account the complexity and number of requests; you will
be informed of any extension, with the reasons for it, within one month of receipt of the request. Requests are free
of charge; where requests are manifestly unfounded or excessive, in particular because of their repetitive character,
YWS may charge a reasonable fee or refuse to act on them (Article 12(5) GDPR).

9.4. As regards the data collected by the embedded map (section 4.2), you may exercise your rights in respect of each
controller (Article 26(3) GDPR). As YWS does not receive those data, requests concerning their further processing are
answered by Google.

### Right to object

**Where YWS processes personal data concerning you on the basis of its legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR),
you have the right to object to that processing at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation (Article
21 GDPR). YWS will then no longer process the data unless it demonstrates compelling legitimate grounds for the
processing which override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing is necessary for the establishment,
exercise or defence of legal claims.**

alleged infringement (Article 77 GDPR). In Luxembourg, the supervisory authority is the Commission nationale pour la
protection des données (CNPD), 1, avenue du Rock'n'roll, L-4361 Esch-sur-Alzette, tel. (+352) 26 10 60-1,
